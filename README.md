# FeedbackCopilot 🎯

### AI-Powered Voice of Customer Platform for Product Managers

FeedbackCopilot is an AI-powered Voice of Customer (VoC) platform designed to help Product Managers turn unstructured customer feedback into clear, actionable product insights.

Instead of manually going through hundreds or thousands of customer reviews, surveys, support tickets, and feedback, FeedbackCopilot analyzes the data and helps identify customer sentiment, recurring themes, pain points, product opportunities, and recommended actions.

## 🚀 Problem

Product Managers receive customer feedback from multiple sources, but analyzing large amounts of unstructured feedback manually can be time-consuming.

The challenge is not only collecting feedback, but understanding:

* What are customers complaining about?
* What problems are repeated most often?
* Which features are customers requesting?
* Which customer problems should receive attention first?
* What actions can the product team take?

## 💡 Solution

FeedbackCopilot uses AI to transform raw customer feedback into a structured product insight workflow:

**Customer Feedback → AI Analysis → Themes → Insights → Opportunities → Actions**

This helps Product Managers move from simply reading feedback to making more informed product decisions.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Feedback Upload

Upload customer feedback and bring it into one place for analysis.

### 2. AI Feedback Analysis

Analyze individual feedback to identify:

* Sentiment
* Topic
* Feedback type
* Customer pain point

### 3. Feedback Explorer

Search, filter, and explore individual customer feedback.

### 4. Theme Detection

Automatically group similar feedback into common customer themes and problems.

### 5. Customer Insights

Convert recurring feedback patterns into clear customer insights supported by feedback evidence.

### 6. Opportunity Prioritization

Identify important product opportunities using factors such as:

* Feedback frequency
* Customer impact
* Business impact
* Trends

### 7. VoC Copilot

Ask questions about customer feedback using an AI-powered conversational interface.

Example questions:

* What are customers complaining about the most?
* What features are requested most often?
* What are the biggest customer pain points?
* What problems should the product team focus on?

### 8. Action Generator

Convert customer insights into potential product actions, user stories, and success metrics.

## 🧠 Product Management Approach

The product follows a simple PM workflow:

```text
Raw Customer Feedback
        ↓
AI Analysis
        ↓
Theme Detection
        ↓
Customer Insights
        ↓
Product Opportunities
        ↓
Prioritization
        ↓
Recommended Actions
        ↓
Success Metrics
```

The goal is to help Product Managers connect **customer voice with product decisions**.

## 🖥️ Example

A customer says:

> "My payment failed twice and I had to try again."

FeedbackCopilot can identify:

```text
Sentiment: Negative
Topic: Payment
Type: Complaint

Pain Point:
Payment failure during checkout
```

When similar feedback is found across many customers, the system can create a broader insight:

```text
Theme:
Payment Problems

Customer Insight:
Customers are frequently experiencing
payment failures during checkout.

Opportunity:
Improve payment reliability.

Suggested Metric:
Payment Success Rate
```
