 FeedbackCopilot 🎯

AI-Powered Voice of Customer Platform

FeedbackCopilot is an AI-powered tool designed to help Product Managers analyze customer feedback and turn it into actionable product insights.

🚀 What It Does

Customer Feedback → AI Analysis → Themes → Insights → Opportunities → Actions

✨ Key Features

📥 **Feedback Upload** – Upload customer feedback for analysis.
  🤖 **AI Analysis** – Detect sentiment, topics, feedback type, and pain points.
  🔍 **Feedback Explorer** – Search and filter customer feedback.
  🧩 **Theme Detection** – Group similar feedback into common themes.
  💡 **Customer Insights** – Identify important customer problems.
  🎯 **Opportunity Prioritization** – Identify which problems need attention.
  💬 **VoC Copilot** – Ask questions about customer feedback using AI.
  ⚡ **Action Generator** – Turn insights into product actions and metrics.

 🎯 Purpose

I built FeedbackCopilot as a Product Management portfolio project to explore how AI can help PMs understand customer needs, identify product opportunities, and make data-informed decisions.
